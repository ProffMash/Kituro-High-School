About Our School

Kituro High School is a proud public boys' and girls' boarding institution set on a quiet hillside, far enough from the town's noise to let learning thrive. Founded decades ago by a small group of community elders determined to give their children a better future, the school has grown from a handful of classrooms under iron-sheet roofs into a modern campus with laboratories, a library, a computer room and playing fields.

Academically, the school has built a reputation for steady improvement in national examinations. Teachers combine disciplined classroom instruction with remedial lessons, group discussions and regular assessment so that every learner, whether gifted or struggling, receives the attention needed to reach his or her potential. Many of our alumni have joined universities and colleges and gone on to become doctors, engineers, teachers, entrepreneurs and public servants.

Beyond textbooks, we believe education must shape the whole person. Students take part in drama and music festivals, science congresses, debating, athletics, football, volleyball, hockey and rugby. Clubs such as Scouts, Young Farmers, Christian Union, Muslim Students' Association and Environmental Club teach leadership, teamwork and service to others.

Character and discipline are at the heart of daily life. From early-morning preps to evening devotions, students learn punctuality, respect, honesty and responsibility. Parents, alumni and the surrounding community remain close partners, supporting the school through the Parents Association, bursaries for needy students and annual tree-planting and clean-up exercises.
